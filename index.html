Our Passion for the Perfect Brew

At Velvet Roast Coffee Lounge, we believe that exceptional coffee starts with exceptional beans. We meticulously source only the finest organic, fair-trade coffee beans from around the globe, ensuring every cup not only tastes exquisite but also supports sustainable practices.

Nestled in the vibrant heart of downtown Seattle, our lounge is designed to be your cozy escape. From the rich aroma of freshly roasted beans to the warm embrace of our inviting atmosphere, we've created a space where you can relax, connect, and truly savor the moment.

Join us for an artisanal coffee experience crafted with care, expertise, and a genuine love for coffee.
